Skepticist

Dance piece Premiere 2025 Maschinenhaus Essen

On the piece

“Skepticist” explores rationality as a core value in building an ideal society.

The story begins with a bold hypothesis: if every individual’s actions were driven by rationality, a spirit of exploration, and critical thinking, could a more ideal society emerge?
