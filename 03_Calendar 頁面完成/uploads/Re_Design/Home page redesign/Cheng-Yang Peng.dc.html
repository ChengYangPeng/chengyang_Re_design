About 關於

一九九九年生於新竹。先學武術與太極，後來才學跳舞。

Cheng-Yang Peng began in martial arts and Tai Chi under Master Wang Yong-Shun — a national Tai Chi champion before he was a dancer. That early discipline of stillness and attention still shapes everything he makes.

He trained at Folkwang University of the Arts in Essen, danced with Aalto Ballet Essen and Ballet Hagen, and performed in the Opening Ceremony of the 2024 Paris Paralympic Games. Since 2025 he works independently, between Germany and Taiwan.

他的編舞像電影：注意取景、時間、與情緒的弧線。受 Alexander Ekman 與 Pina Bausch 影響，但更相信身體本來的重量。

Repertoire — Ekman · Cranko · Goecke · O'Day · Bausch · Konjetzky · Brinkmann
